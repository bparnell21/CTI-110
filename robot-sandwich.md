# Operation Turkey Sandwitch

## Assumptions

- The robot is sitting at a kitchen table 
- The robot has two empty hands
- A full loaf of Hawaiian Bread is inide a container on the counter
- The bread container has a lid on it
- A sealed package of Sliced Turkey is on the counter
- A sealed package of cheddar cheese is on the counter
- A jar of mayonaise is on the counter
- A clean butter knife is on the counter
- A plastic plate is on the counter
- The robot can identify everything by its label.
- The robot can stand, walk, sit down, grab, lift, hold, move, twist, turn, and release objects

## Steps

- Stand up and walk towards the counter where the bread is
- stop when you are standing directly in front of the counter
- move your left hand towards the breasd container 
- open the bread container by removing the top
- put the top on the counter
- use you right hand to pick up two slices of the Hawaiian Bread
- place the bread on the paper plate beside one another
- pick up the top of the bread container with your left hand
- place the top of container on top of the bread container
- use your right hand to pick up the jar of mayonaise
- place your left hand on the bottom of the jar
- place your right hand on the lid of the jar
- Twist the top of the mayonaise with your right hand until it it loose enough to come off
- pick up the lid with your right hand and place it on the counter
- place the jar of mayonaise on the counter
- pick up the butter knife with your right hand
- slide the knife inside the mayonaise jar with your right hand
- pull some of the mayonaise out of the jar useing the knife
- pick up one of the slices of bread using your left hand
- slide the knife across the bread
- place the bread back onto the plate
- slide the knife back into the mayonaise jar using the same hand
- pull some of the mayonaise out of the jar useing the knife
- pick the other slice of bread up
- slide the knife across the bread
- place the bread back onto the plate
- put the knife down on the counter
- use your right hand to pick up the package of cheese
- take your left hand and place it on the right top corner of the sealed cheese
- pull the seal back to open the cheese
- place the package of cheese on the counter 
- use your left hand to pick up a piece of cheese out of the cheese package
- place the cheese on one of the bread slices
- take your left hand and place it on the top corner of the sealed turkey package
- pull the seal back to open the turkey
- place the package of turkey on the counter 
- use your left hand to pick up a piece of turkey out of the turkey package
- place the turkey on top of the cheese
- pick up the slice of bread that only has mayonaise on it using your right hand
- place it down on top of slice pf bread that has the turkey and cheese on it
- grab the package of cheese with your left hand
- use your right hand to pull the seal back to the right top corner of the package
- place the cheese package on the counter
- grab the package of turkey with your left hand
- use your right hand to pull the seal back to the right top corner of the package
- place the turkey package on the counter 
- pick up the jar of mayonaise with your right hand
- pick up the lid to the jar of mayonaise with your left hand
- place the lid down on the top of the jar
- twist the lid until it is tight
- put the mayonaise jar on the counter
